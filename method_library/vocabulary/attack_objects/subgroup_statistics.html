额外检索线索

  • 2017 A review of statistical disclosure control techniques employed by web-based data query systems (J Public Health Management and Practice)
  • 强调 subgroup statistics 在交互式 query 系统里会因 differencing / suppression flaw 泄露。
  • 2003 Revealing Information while Preserving Privacy (PODS)
  • 虽然不是专写 subgroup statistics，但它是 subgroup/aggregate reconstruction 的奠基文献。
  • 2011 Data confidentiality: A review of methods for statistical disclosure limitation and methods for assessing privacy (Statistics Surveys)
  • 统计领域的高价值综述，适合沿其参考文献继续追 subgroup statistics、表格发布和 disclosure limitation。
  • 2009 Statistical Disclosure Control for Survey Data (Handbook of Statistics)
  • 更直接地讨论总量、均值、比例和表格发布中的 confidentiality 风险，是 subgroup_statistics 的统计学主干文献。
  • 2018 Understanding Database Reconstruction Attacks on Public Data (ACM Queue)
  • 这篇文章很适合补在 subgroup_statistics 页，因为它把聚合表、公开统计与重构攻击之间的关系讲得非常直观。
